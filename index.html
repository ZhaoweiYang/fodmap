    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is GutWise really free?

    Yes. The core toolkit — meal & symptom logging, the essential food library, weekly trends and simple flare patterns — is free forever, not a trial. Premium adds the AI and human-expert layers on top, and you can try it free for 7 days.

    What is the low-FODMAP diet?

    FODMAPs are short-chain carbohydrates — found in foods like garlic, onion, wheat and many fruits — that ferment in the gut and can trigger IBS symptoms. The low-FODMAP approach is a structured, three-phase way to find your triggers: eliminate for 2–6 weeks, reintroduce group by group, then personalize. In clinical studies, around three in four people with IBS report meaningful relief. We recommend doing it with a healthcare professional — GutWise makes the day-to-day tracking part easy.

    Is GutWise medical advice?

    No. GutWise is an educational and tracking tool, not a medical device, and it doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. Always talk to your doctor or a registered dietitian before changing your diet — and see a doctor promptly for alarm symptoms like blood in your stool, unexplained weight loss, fever or severe persistent pain.

    How does the AI trigger analysis work?

    Premium's AI looks across everything you log — meals, symptom timing, sleep, stress — and ranks the foods and habits most strongly associated with your flares, with a confidence level for each. It typically needs about two weeks of logging to surface its first patterns, and it gets sharper the longer you track.

    Do I have to follow the low-FODMAP diet to use GutWise?

    Not at all. Plenty of members just use the logging, trends and trigger analysis. The three-phase plan is there if and when you and your clinician decide it's the right move.

    Is my health data private?

    Your logs are encrypted in transit and at rest, we never sell personal data, and you can export or permanently delete everything at any time. Doctor reports are only generated when you ask for them.

    Can I cancel Premium anytime?

    Yes — one tap in settings, no emails to support, no retention hoops. Your free plan and all your data stay intact.
